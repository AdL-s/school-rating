Smíchovská střední škola

Prague SPŠ Public

A public technical secondary school in Prague-Smíchov, founded in 1901. It teaches information technology and cyber security alongside hospitality, tourism and general lyceum programmes. Students praise the modern IT labs and teachers, but mention crowded corridors between lessons.
