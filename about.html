About SchoolScore

Honest school reviews from anyone who knows the school.

Choosing a secondary school shouldn’t be a guess.

Every year thousands of fourteen-year-olds in the Czech Republic pick a gymnázium, SPŠ or obchodní akademie after a single open day and a few rumours. Official statistics tell you exam results, but not what it feels like to sit in the classroom.

SchoolScore collects reviews from the people who actually know: students, parents, graduates and teachers. No account, no paywall, no paid placements. Just real experiences.

Fair to readers and to schools.

Ratings only matter if people trust them. These rules apply to every review on the site.

No account needed

Anyone who knows a school can review it. We never ask for an email or a phone number.

No paid rankings

Schools can’t pay to move up or hide reviews. The ranking is the average rating, nothing more.
